Write \(\delta=\delta(N)=q+2p+1\). \begin{enumerate} \item Every prime of \(N\) has exponent at most~3. \item If \(r_1\ne r_2\) lie in \(R\) and \(N\) has a prime factor \(t\notin\{r_1,r_2\}\) (for instance if \(j\ge 1\)), then \(q>5p^2-4p-3\). \item \(|R|\le 2\). \item If \(j\ge 1\) and \(R=\{r_1,r_2\}\), then both exponents equal~2, \(r_1r_2\mid L(p)\), and each \(r_i\) satisfies \(r_i^2\ge N/\delta>\sqrt{L(p)}\). \item Any prime of exponent~3 satisfies \(r^2\mid L(p)\) (hence \(r<p^{3/2}\)); after excluding \(j=0\) via Theorems~\ref{thm:closed} and~\ref{thm:B}, this occurs only for \(|R|=1\). \end{enumerate} Consequently every remaining repeated-prime solution has shape \(r^2V\) or \(r^3V\) with \(V\) squarefree of \(\omega(V)=j\ge 2\), or shape \(r_1^2r_2^2V\) with \(r_1r_2\mid L(p)\) and \(q>5p^2-4p-3\).

## lem:G paper/p2q-jumps-v1.4.tex:2249 | map lean: ['lemmaG', 'lemmaG_gcd', 'T_pos'] | disp: proved
[Lemma~G] Under~\eqref{eq:jumps}, writing \(T(p)=p^4-13p-6\), \[ p(p-1)\sig(N)-(p^2+p+1)\ph(N)=2T(p). \] In particular \(\gcd(\ph(N),\sig(N))\mid 2T(p)\). Moreover \(T(p)>0\) for every prime \(p\ge 5\).

## thm:F paper/p2q-jumps-v1.4.tex:2266 | map lean: ['theoremF'] | disp: proved
[Theorem~F] Assume~\eqref{eq:jumps}. Let \(o\) be the number of odd exponents in the prime factorization of \(N\). Then \(2^o\mid 2T(p)\), equivalently \(o\le 1+v_2(T(p))\). When \(o\ge 1\) one has \(2^{o-1}\mid T(p)\). For squarefree \(N\) one has \(o=\omega(N)=k\), so \(2^{k-1}\mid T(p)\).
