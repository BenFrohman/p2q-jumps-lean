# Hypothesis R3 — updated status (November 2026)

## Mixed square N = r^{2} s t

Unknowns: distinct primes p, q, r, s, t ≥ 5.

The jump identities force the explicit formulae for σ = s + t, π = s t, and q given in the paper (equations (5)–(7)).

### Q < 0 half-plane (p < r) is empty (theorem)

The discriminant Δ_st = σ^{2} - 4π has numerator H(p,r) (degree 7 in each variable) and denominator r^{2}(p - r) Q(p,r)^{2}.

For p < r the denominator is negative. After the change of variables p = 5 + u, r = 11 + u + v (u, v ≥ 0 covering 5 ≤ p ≤ r - 6), the expanded form of H has 50 monomials, every coefficient strictly positive (smallest coefficient +4, constant term 91 908 696). Thus H(p,r) > 0 throughout the region, hence Δ_st < 0. No real complementary primes s, t exist.

The boundary lines r = p + 2 and r = p + 4 are already excluded by offset arguments. Therefore the entire half-plane Q(p,r) < 0 is empty.

### Residual: p > r (Q > 0)

The condition reduces to the single-variable divisibility Q(p,r) | F(r), where
F(r) = r^{7} + 4 r^{6} + r^{5} - r^{4} - 10 r^{3} - 37 r^{2} - 72 r - 48,
together with the congruence Q ≡ -6 (mod r + 2) and Q odd.

Computational search under this reduced condition finds zero solutions for all primes r ≤ 20 000.

## ω(N) ≥ 4

Remains residual. The product bound on A(N)/N does not yield a uniform contradiction (the inequality direction allows A/N 	o 0 for large primes). A δ-matching bootstrap may still be possible but is not yet complete.

## Summary for Conjecture 3 (n = p^{2} q)

- R1 (N = rst): theorem (empty).
- R2 (N = r^{3} s): computationally empty for all primes p ≤ 10^{5} (elementary proof for simple-root Hensel lifts still open).
- R3 (N = r^{2} s t): p < r proved empty; p > r empty to r = 20 000 under Q | F(r).
- ω(N) ≥ 4: residual.
