# Hypothesis R3 — explicit equations (open)

## Mixed square N = r^2 s t

Unknowns: distinct primes p, q, r, s, t ≥ 5.

Write σ = s + t and π = s t. The jump identities force

    σ = -(p^3 - p^2 r - p^2 + 2 p r^3 + p r^2 - 2 p r + r^3 - r^2 - 12)
          / (r (p r + 2 p - r^2 + r))

    π = (2 p^4 r + p^4 - 2 p^3 r^2 - p^3 + 2 p^2 r^4 - p^2 r^2 - p^2 r
           - 24 p r - 12 p + 12 r^2 - 12 r)
          / (r (p - r) (p r + 2 p - r^2 + r))

    q = (2 p^2 r^2 + p^2 r - 2 p r^3 - p r + 2 r^5 - r^3 - r^2 - 12 r - 24)
          / ((p - r) (p r + 2 p - r^2 + r))

Necessary: denominators nonzero, σ > 0, π > 0, σ^2 - 4π a positive perfect square,
s, t = (σ ± sqrt(σ^2-4π))/2 distinct primes different from p,q,r, and q prime.

Closed lines: r in {p-6, p-4, p-2, p+2, p+6, p+8} (sign or negative discriminant).
r = p+4 is open only for p ≡ 5, 7, 13, 15 (mod 16).

## Four-prime N = r s t u

Let e1 = r+s+t+u, e2 = sum of pairwise products, e3 = sum of triple products, e4 = r s t u.
Then e4 = p^2 q + 12 and

    e2 = p + (q-1)/2
    e3 = p^2 + p q + (q-1)/2 - e1 + 1

AM-GM: e2 ≥ 6 sqrt(N). Balanced four-tuples with q = O(p) are incompatible.
After fixing two small primes r < s, the complementary pair (t,u) solves a quadratic
whose coefficients depend on (p,r,s). That is a family of forms, not one polynomial in p.

## ω(N) ≥ 5

The same elementary-symmetric count: three constraints, ω-1 free prime parameters.
No uniform size obstruction of T/N type has been proved.
